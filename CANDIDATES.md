# Candidates — One Line, One Laugh

Eight candidates generated by DeepSeek (via 100milionseek, brutal mode,
one generation call; the follow-up critique calls failed — DeepSeek
returned HTTP 503/524 — so the critique-pick below is the builder's own,
against the kit's judge criteria in order: 1) a funny line, 2) a real
turn/surprise, 3) tight, 4) fresh, 5) clear on first read. Gate: 1–140
characters, single line.)

1. (81) "My therapist said I avoid intimacy, so I moved in with her and now we don't talk."
   — Neat ironic reversal, clear and tight. But the laugh is mild: the
   twist is a shrug, not a scene change.
2. (88) "The funeral home lost my dad's ashes, so we buried an urn full of my mother's potpourri."
   — "Potpourri" is a vivid, specific word and the twist is strong. But
   it leans on a dead father and risks reading as mean rather than funny.
3. (107) "I told my landlord the ceiling leaks; he said \"that's not a leak, that's the apartment above you existing.\""
   — Relatable, deadpan landlord reframe. Longest of the set and the
   punch ("existing") is a familiar landlord trope.
4. (98) "My gym charges extra for the sauna, which is just a closet with warm towels and a man's lost hope."
   — "A man's lost hope" is a nice image, but the line is an observation
   with a garnish, not a twist.
5. (84) "I clapped when the plane landed and the pilot announced, \"Sir, this is a Greyhound.\"" — **PICKED**
   — The last four words reframe the entire scene: no plane, no pilot,
   no landing, just a bus and a man clapping at the driver. Concrete,
   visual, surprising, tight (84 chars), fresh, and clear on first read.
   Strongest on criterion 2 (the turn) by a clear margin.
6. (104) "My dog learned to open the fridge, then spent three days pretending nothing happened and gaining weight."
   — Cute and true, but loose: the twist ("pretending nothing happened")
   is mild and the line runs long.
7. (88) "I went to a silent meditation retreat and got kicked out for narrating my own breathing."
   — Runner-up. Crisp, specific, funny image, clear on first read. Its
   twist is one behaviour, not a whole-scene reframe, so it loses to #5
   on criterion 2.
8. (96) "My grandmother's dying wish was to be cremated, so we honored it by making her a lovely lasagna."
   — Shocking twist, but the joke is built on a dead grandmother; a
   judge can read it as mean, and "lasagna" as a cremation substitute
   is absurd without being earned.
